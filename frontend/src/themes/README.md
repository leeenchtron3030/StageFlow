# Themes

## Purpose

`themes` is reserved for future white-label theme architecture.

## What Belongs Here

- Theme contracts.
- Theme mapping utilities.
- Future white-label theme definitions after approval.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Alternate themes in ED-0003.
- Event-specific branding before a future directive approves it.
- Workflow-specific styling.

## Expected Future Directives

- Future white-label or design-system directives may populate this directory.
